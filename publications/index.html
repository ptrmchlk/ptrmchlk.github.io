  • Michalak, P., Duneau, D., & Ferdy, J.-B. 2026. Seminal fluid proteins as regulation factors for optimizing reproduction: a modeling approach. The American Naturalist, 208(1):83–100 doi: 10.1086/740809 | code

    In the past few years I focused on the evolution of seminal fluid proteins. Those proteins are provided by males to females during mating in many animal species. They became a poster example of traits males use to manipulate females and evolving primarily under sexual conflict. Yet, the work we did, and the models we created in the above papers, suggests that under some circumstances, those proteins are rather beneficial to females and allow for adjusting their reproduction.
