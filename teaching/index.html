Student projects

As a temporary staff, I do not have student projects to offer. Projects can also get delayed, analysis and writing postponed, and in such case, you should have someone permanent at the university you can reach out to.

However. I am always happy to discuss your project ideas. I can also offer support in finding a staff member interested in co-supervision of a shared project. So, if you are interested in the evolution of sex, mating, sexual selection, modelling, or social behaviour in general feel free to reach out!
